## Execution graph (published results)

`analyze.py` reads the three `terapeak_*.csv` files, filters to standalone vaporizers,
assigns `period`/`period_detailed`, computes Mann–Whitney U, Welch's t, Cohen's d, and
Kruskal–Wallis, then writes `data/combined_cleaned.csv`, `data/statistics_summary.csv`,
`data/regulatory_timeline.csv`, and `figures/fig1–fig6.{png,pdf}`.

`create_jca_tables.py` reads `combined_cleaned.csv` + `statistics_summary.csv`, recomputes
Spearman/Kendall/quarterly trend tests, and writes `papers/jca_table{1,2,s1}.docx`.

`write_jca_english.py` reads the same generated data + figures and writes
`papers/jca_manuscript_english.docx`.
